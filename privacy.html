Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Information we collect

We collect contact and business details, website URLs, preferences, correspondence, uploaded content and project information you provide. Payment details are handled by Stripe; we may receive payment status and billing information, not full card details through our website.

How information is used

We use information to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, deliver projects, process billing, provide support and maintain business records.

Service providers

Our site uses GitHub Pages for hosting, Formspree for visitor contact submissions, Zite for post-purchase customer project intake and Stripe for payments. These services may process information under their own privacy policies. We may use suitable design and content tools to deliver projects; avoid submitting sensitive material or credentials.

Phone numbers and text messages

If you provide a phone number, we use it to respond to your inquiry or arrange a requested consultation. If you choose text-message confirmation and check the separate SMS consent box, we may send scheduling-related text messages to that number. SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchasing services. Message and data rates may apply; reply STOP to opt out. We do not use this consent to send promotional text messages.

Retention and security

We retain information as reasonably needed for service delivery, support, business records and legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information, subject to applicable law and necessary recordkeeping. You may decline optional information, although it may affect our ability to deliver services.

Technical data and external links

Hosting and embedded third-party services may collect technical information such as IP addresses or use cookies under their own policies. External websites have separate privacy practices.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use our Contact page.
