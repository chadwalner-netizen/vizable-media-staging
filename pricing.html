Package details, ownership & billing terms

Monthly plans: Your initial payment ($184 for Starter, $254 for Business, or $396 for Premium) covers website creation and your first 90 days of hosting and maintenance. After 90 days, automatic monthly billing begins at $59, $79, or $99 per month, respectively. Monthly service includes hosting, routine security and maintenance, plus up to 30 minutes of minor content edits each month. Unused edit time does not roll over. Cancel anytime; the hosted subscription site goes offline at the end of the paid service period unless purchased outright.

One-time plans: The listed price covers the agreed website and transfer of its files, subject to third-party asset licenses. Domain registration, hosting, ongoing changes and maintenance are separate; optional managed hosting and maintenance is $39/month. You retain your business content and domains registered in your name.

New pages, extensive redesigns, specialized functionality, or requests outside package scope require a separate agreement. Every purchase is subject to project-scope confirmation; if it isn't a fit, you may choose an upgrade or receive a full refund before design work begins. See our refund policy.
