Getting Started

What happens after I purchase a website?+

After checkout, complete our client intake questionnaire. We’ll review your materials and contact you within 24–48 hours to confirm scope and next steps. We’ll then send three customized homepage design concepts for you to choose from before development begins.

How long does it take to build my website?+

Most websites are completed within an estimated 2–4 business days after written homepage design approval and receipt of all required materials. This is not a guaranteed deadline.

What information do you need from me?+

Your business name, services, contact details, branding preferences, domain information and any content or media you have.

What if I don’t have written content or photographs?+

We can create standard website copy using your business information and source suitable licensed or AI-generated imagery at no extra charge. Extensive copywriting, custom graphics, photography and specialized media may require a separate quote. You approve the content.

Design & Development

What is included in each website package?+

Starter, Business and Premium include 3, 5 and 7 pages respectively. Every package receives the same design quality and attention to detail. Standard inclusions: Three customized homepage concepts, one round of minor mockup adjustments, professional mobile-friendly design, standard copy and image sourcing, image enhancement, embedded videos, contact forms, social links, basic on-page SEO, pre-launch functionality testing and two hours of reasonable post-build revisions within scope. Advanced functionality such as custom databases, e-commerce, membership systems, complex booking and specialized integrations requires a separate quote. See our Pricing page.

What features can you include?+

Navigation menus, contact forms, enhanced images, embedded video and social links, depending on scope. Payment integration costs extra. Domain connection is included with active hosting plans.

Can I review my design before development?+

Absolutely! We’ll create three customized homepage design concepts based on your business, branding and preferences. Choose your favorite, request one round of minor adjustments and approve the design in writing before development begins. Additional interior-page mockups may be provided when appropriate.

What if I’m not satisfied with my finished website?+

We include up to two hours of total revision work within the original scope after the site is built. Additional work or major redesigns require an approved quote.

What if I delay approving my design?+

We’ll make up to four attempts to contact you regarding design approval. If we receive no response, your project may be placed on hold until we hear from you. Work resumes according to availability. After 60 days, scope and timing may need review and additional work may incur an agreed reactivation fee.

Pricing & Ownership

Can I purchase my website outright?+

Yes. Our one-time packages are $499, $749 and $999 for 3, 5 and 7 pages. Website files are included, subject to third-party licenses. Hosting and domain registration are separate.

What if I need fewer or more pages than your standard packages?+

One-page websites start at $249, with each additional standard page costing $125. Custom page counts are available by request and separately invoiced. Nonstandard monthly packages require a custom quote. Advanced functionality is priced separately. Contact us.

How do monthly website plans work?+

Upfront payments of $184, $254 or $396 cover development and the initial 90-day service period starting on the purchase date. Automatic billing then begins at $59, $79 or $99 per month respectively. Customer delays do not extend the period or postpone billing. We may make appropriate adjustments for substantial delays caused by us.

Do I own my website and domain?+

Outright buyers receive the agreed website files subject to third-party licenses. Your business content remains yours. Domains registered in your name belong to you. Subscription websites remain part of our hosted service until bought out. Fixed buyout prices are $299.40 (Starter), $449.40 (Business) and $599.40 (Premium), regardless of subscription duration. Contact us to arrange a buyout.

What is your refund policy?+

Before design work begins, a full refund is available. After mockups are created but before development begins, refunds are reduced by $75, $100 or $125 for Starter, Business or Premium. After approved design and development begins, change-of-mind refunds generally are not offered. If we cannot deliver the agreed project, an appropriate refund will be considered. See Terms & Conditions.

Hosting & Support

What is included in the $39/month Hosting & Maintenance plan?+

Outright buyers can add hosting, routine maintenance, technical support and 30 minutes of minor updates monthly. You may also use your own hosting.

Can I purchase hosting later?+

Yes. The Hosting & Maintenance plan is available at checkout or later, exclusively to customers who purchased a Vizable Media website outright.

Can I request changes after launch?+

Active hosting plans include 30 minutes of minor updates per month, with no rollover. Additional work is $75/hour, subject to prior approval; major projects may require a separate quote.

Can I use an existing domain?+

Yes. We encourage you to use your own domain. Connection is included with active hosting plans; registration and renewal fees are separate.

What if I don’t have a domain?+

We can guide you through registration free of charge, or handle standard registration and setup for $50 for the first year (includes a domain costing up to $20). Premium domains and renewals cost extra. We prefer registration in your name; any managed registration and transfer terms will be agreed in writing.

What happens if I cancel my monthly subscription?+

Cancel through Stripe or contact us for assistance. Your subscription website goes offline at the end of the paid period unless you arrange a buyout. Contact us before cancellation to discuss transfer and hosting.

Additional Services

Can you add online payments or e-commerce?+

Yes. Basic payment-link integration (1–3 options) is $129; expanded integration (4–10 options) is $249. Full e-commerce requires a custom quote. These are separate one-time setup fees, available for new or compatible existing sites, subject to review. We invoice approved work through Stripe. You need your own payment processor; third-party fees are separate.

Can you improve my existing website?+

Yes, subject to platform compatibility and access. We can review Wix, WordPress and other sites, recommend improvements or rebuild a site with new code. Request a free preliminary website review and custom quote.

What if my project is outside the package?+

We confirm scope before work begins. You can approve an upgrade or custom quote, or receive a full refund if the project is not a fit before design work begins.

Can I schedule a phone or Zoom call?+

Yes. Contact us before, during or after development with a preferred day, a 2–3-hour availability window and callback number or Zoom preference.
