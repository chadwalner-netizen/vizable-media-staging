Scope and approval

Advanced functionality such as custom databases, e-commerce, membership systems, complex booking and specialized integrations requires a separate quote. Custom one-time page counts start at $249 for one standard page, plus $125 for each additional standard page. Nonstandard monthly packages and refund deductions for custom projects are agreed in writing. Three customized homepage concepts and one round of minor adjustments are included before written approval. Development estimates of 2–4 business days begin after approval and receipt of required materials; they are not guaranteed. Customers approve content and must have rights to supplied materials.

Payments and subscriptions

Prices are stated on the Pricing page. Monthly packages begin at purchase and include development and the initial 90-day period; recurring billing begins after 90 days at the selected rate. Customer delays do not postpone billing. Substantial delays caused by us may warrant an appropriate adjustment. Optional $39 hosting for outright buyers bills monthly starting at enrollment. Cancel through Stripe or contact us for assistance; service continues through the paid period.

Refunds

Full refund before design work begins. After mockups are created but before development starts, the refund is reduced by $75 (Starter), $100 (Business) or $125 (Premium). After approved design and development begins, change-of-mind refunds generally are not offered. If we cannot deliver the agreed scope, an appropriate refund will be determined based on circumstances. Mandatory legal rights are unaffected.

Revisions and delays

Up to two hours of post-build revisions within scope are included. Active hosting includes 30 minutes of minor edits monthly, without rollover. Extra work is $75/hour or separately quoted and approved. We make up to four contact attempts regarding design approval. Unanswered projects may be placed on hold and rescheduled according to availability. After 60 days of inactivity, scope and schedule may be reviewed; any necessary reactivation fee requires agreement. Payments are not automatically forfeited.

Hosting is subject to third-party availability; uninterrupted service is not guaranteed.
